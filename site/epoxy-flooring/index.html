Process

Precision in every layer.

Epoxy is only as good as its prep. We spend most of our time on the part you never see.

01

Assess

Free on-site visit. We test moisture, check the slab and recommend the right system for your budget.

02

Prep

Diamond grinding and shot-blasting open the pores so the coating bonds like it was poured with the slab.

03

Coat

Primer, color or flake broadcast, then clear topcoat. Each layer is timed to cure at its best.

04

Cure & seal

We walk the floor, handle the cure window and hand over care instructions. Then we stand behind it.

FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

How long does an epoxy floor take?

Most residential garages are finished in 2–3 days. Commercial and industrial jobs are scheduled around your operation, often after hours or in phases.

Can you install over my existing concrete?

In most cases, yes. We diagnose the slab first, test for moisture, and repair cracks or spalling before coating. Some floors need a moisture-mitigating primer.

Is epoxy slippery?

Not when installed correctly. We add anti-slip aggregate or a textured topcoat, and can match the grip level to your use case.

When can I park or walk on it?

Light foot traffic is usually fine within 24 hours. Vehicles typically wait 72 hours, and heavy equipment needs the full cure window. We confirm the exact timing for your system.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Every install includes a written warranty covering delamination and workmanship. Details are provided with your quote.
